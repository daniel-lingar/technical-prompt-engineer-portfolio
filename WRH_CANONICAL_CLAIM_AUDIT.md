# WRH Canonical Claim Audit

## Purpose

This record distinguishes claims that are presently supported by public sources from claims that need a primary document, an owner-approved statement, or correction before they appear in the proof-of-work portfolio.

## Initial Findings

| Claim area | Current evidence | Publication status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WRH purpose and non-clinical scope | The public WRH landing page describes a structured, non-clinical education system, its intended high-barrier audiences, no-disclosure learning, safety-aware delivery, and explicit non-treatment boundary. | **Supported by public site.** Cite the WRH site directly. |
| WRH session count | The public curriculum index explicitly states that the source repository contains **77 session files**, preserved in the searchable explorer. The supplied narrative states a 76-session structure. The public index is the current canonical source for public-facing copy. | **Use 77 source sessions** in public materials unless the owner republishes a corrected canonical index. |
| Rescue Boat publication structure | The public landing page identifies a ten-article edition, a version lock (`v1.1.2`), and its connection to WRH and Capitol Contracts. | **Supported by public site.** |
| Working manuscript role | The public manuscript page identifies the text as a working manuscript. It contains sensitive lived-experience material. | **Supported, but do not use as initial evaluation input.** |
| Capitol Contracts public role | The public landing page identifies Capitol Contracts LLC as an educational-support-resources organization and states its non-clinical boundary. It shows William Dane Lingar as CEO/President/Majority Owner. | **Use only the published role wording.** Do not publish unverified ownership percentages or additional titles without an owner-approved public source. |
| VA 2023 veteran-suicide statistic | The official VA report states that 6,398 Veterans died by suicide in 2023, an average of 17.5 per day, and that 61% were not receiving VA health care in the final year of life. | **Supported by official source**, but use with a direct citation and do not imply that WRH causes, prevents, diagnoses, or treats suicide risk. |
| Five-year revenue, margin, and participant projections | Provided in the supplied narrative but not found in the public sources reviewed so far. | **Internal / owner-review only** until a public, approved business-plan source is designated. |

## Safe Portfolio Positioning

The AI portfolio should frame its first project as a narrowly scoped **evidence-bound source extraction and uncertainty-handling evaluation**, using public archive materials. It should not claim clinical efficacy, suicide prevention outcomes, regulatory approval, funded contract status, ownership percentages, or business projections.
