# Design Brainstorm — Technical Prompt Engineer & AI Evaluation Specialist

## Three Candidate Directions

### 1. Evidence Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial research dossier that makes reasoning visible through annotations, numbered evidence, and disciplined hierarchy. It feels like an evaluation artifact rather than a conventional personal site.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Signal Workshop
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, technical studio built around “inputs → method → measurable output.” It presents prompt work as careful craft and operational rigor rather than generative spectacle.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Calibration Lab
**Very Brief Intro:** A restrained scientific interface inspired by annotated lab notebooks and model-evaluation scorecards. It uses clear visual calibration cues to signal precision, repeatability, and human judgment.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Direction: Calibration Lab

### Design Movement
**Contemporary scientific editorial**: laboratory notebook restraint combined with data-visualization clarity and high-quality technical publishing.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence precedes assertion:** Each capability is framed by its method, schema, or rubric rather than broad claims.
2. **Precision is visual:** Numbered markers, linework, labels, and calibrated scales make the portfolio feel auditable.
3. **Human-readable rigor:** Complex evaluation concepts remain scannable for both an automated screener and an AI-operations leader.
4. **Useful density:** Deliberate negative space separates structured information without turning the experience into an empty marketing page.

### Color Philosophy
The interface uses a quiet **porcelain paper** ground to communicate clarity and a deep **graphite ink** for document-grade authority. A saturated **calibration vermilion** is reserved for critical markers, decision points, and proof of work; it should feel like a reviewer’s precise annotation, not a decorative accent. Muted blue-gray supports metadata and system labels without competing with the work itself.

### Layout Paradigm
The site follows a **folio-and-margin** structure rather than a centralized card grid. A persistent left rail contains the identity, navigation, and document metadata; the right reading field changes scale between editorial passages, annotated schemas, and curriculum timelines. At narrow widths, the rail becomes a compact top dossier header.

### Signature Elements
1. **Calibration ticks:** Short horizontal rules and number markers organize sections and content depth.
2. **Reviewer annotations:** Vermilion brackets, small caps labels, and callouts identify methods and outcomes.
3. **Schema panes:** Monospaced, lightly tinted panels translate prompt templates into immediately inspectable artifacts.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like handling a precise document. Hover states reveal underlines, contextual labels, and restrained highlights. Navigation scrolls to evidence without obscuring the reading position; all links are obvious, accessible, and keyboard-friendly.

### Animation
Motion is low-amplitude and utility-led: a 180–240ms custom ease-out for navigation indicators, annotation reveals, and proof tiles. Content may enter in brief staggered fades and a 6px vertical rise on initial load; all decorative movement is removed under reduced-motion preferences. Buttons use a subtle 0.97 active scale to confirm intent.

### Typography System
**DM Mono** carries metadata, metrics, schema labels, and code-like prompt examples. **Source Serif 4** carries long-form judgment, application copy, and case-study language. **Manrope** provides compact sans-serif headings and interface controls. Headings use Manrope at high contrast, never all-caps except compact labels; body copy maintains comfortable measure and clear paragraph rhythm.

### Brand Essence
**Technical prompt engineering and AI evaluation artifacts for teams that need reliable human judgment at model scale.**

Personality: **methodical, discerning, lucid.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct, evidence-first, and specific. CTAs invite review of a concrete artifact rather than generic conversion language. Microcopy should sound like a capable evaluator documenting a decision.

Example lines:
- “Build prompts that make a judgment reproducible.”
- “Open the rubric before you open the model.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a **calibration aperture**: three imperfectly concentric, offset square brackets forming a precise central target. It is a bold vermilion graphic with no text and can pair with a custom monospaced wordmark built from carefully tracked letterforms.

### Signature Brand Color
**Calibration Vermilion — `#D64E32`**
