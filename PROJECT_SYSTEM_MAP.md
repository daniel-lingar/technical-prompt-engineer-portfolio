# Project System Map

This public portfolio represents one connected system of source organization, plain-language translation, curriculum design, implementation documentation, research packaging, and technical publishing. The repositories are intentionally distinct because they preserve different stages and audiences of the work.

## How the Work Connects

The common method is simple: preserve the source base; separate drafts from canonical outputs; label scope and uncertainty; transform complex material into clear structures for different audiences; use version controls and checks; and publish artifacts that others can inspect.

For recruiter review, begin with the technical portfolio, then inspect the WRH Master Curriculum and the Johnson County Civil War Archive. The book repositories provide supporting evidence of long-form revision control and source-to-publication workflows, but are not required first reads for general technical roles.

## Scope Boundary

The WRH and Rescue Boat materials are non-clinical public education, not therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, legal advice, or crisis care. The technical portfolio demonstrates public prompt and evaluation artifacts; it does not claim employer-sponsored model training, paid RLHF annotation, or clinical credentials.
